01 / Links and fit

How to check ACBuy product links

Open the original Weidian, Taobao or 1688 listing. Verify the seller name, color option, size availability and whether a set includes both pieces.

For sneakers, compare foot length with the listing’s shoe size chart and request an insole measurement if you are between sizes. For hoodies and tees, measure the chest width and body length of a garment you already wear. For pants, check waist, rise and inseam.

02 / Warehouse inspection

What to look for in ACBuy QC photos

Quality-control photos help check the item received at a warehouse. Ask for outsole and heel photos on sneakers, cuff and zipper close-ups on jackets, or clasp and pendant photos on jewelry.

Compare both shoes, read the size label and ask for a ruler photo when dimensions matter. If the color or size is wrong, check the seller’s exchange window before the parcel ships.

03 / Parcel planning

ACBuy shipping guide for a mixed parcel

Budget for the item price, domestic delivery, agent fees, international postage and any applicable destination taxes or duties. Compare currency conversion and the payment provider’s fees when checking the total.

Ask for packed weight and box dimensions. Some routes bill by volumetric weight, so a bulky shoe box can affect the quote. Check delivery estimates, tracking and route restrictions for your destination.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find shoes using an ACBuy spreadsheet?
Start in Shoes, shortlist a model and colorway, then check the original seller link for current stock and measurements.
What should I do if a spreadsheet link is broken?
Search by the model name or style code. Compare the seller, variant and size chart again before saving a replacement link.
Can QC photos establish brand authenticity?
QC photos document visible condition, labels and details. Confirm authenticity through an authorized retailer or a qualified authenticator.
When can I estimate the final international shipping cost?
Compare quotes once you have the packed weight, parcel dimensions and destination. Check what each quote includes and whether duties or taxes may be collected separately.
